8 August 2026 · New publication

A multi-phase reference matching algorithm for bibliometric analysis

Out in Scientometrics: an unsupervised, seven-phase algorithm that consolidates cited-reference variants with no training data, no external APIs, and no network access. By integrating the ISO 4 LTWA directly into the pipeline, it recovers citations that journal-name fragmentation had scattered. Already available in bibliometrix and Biblioshiny.

17 July 2026 · New publication

ThemeScope: mapping social representations in digital arenas

Our new quantitative thematic-analysis method, ThemeScope, has been published in Journal of Information Science. It builds sentence-level co-occurrence networks and maps thematic communities onto a strategic diagram grounded in Social Representation Theory, and comes with the open-source themescopeR package.
