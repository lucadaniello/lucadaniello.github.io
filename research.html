Research Areas & Interests Publications Research Projects

Research Areas & Interests

Bibliometrics & Scientometrics

Development and application of quantitative methods for the analysis of scientific literature, including science mapping, citation analysis, and performance indicators for research institutions.

Text Mining & NLP

Quantitative and qualitative analysis of textual corpora using statistical approaches: topic modeling, thematic evolution, automatic summarization, and knowledge extraction from scientific literature.

Statistics for Healthcare

Evaluation of the scientific productivity, governance, and healthcare performance of Italian public Academic Medical Centers (AHSCs) using bibliometric and statistical indicators.

Statistical Methods

Development and application of advanced statistical and data science methods, from multivariate analysis to machine learning, in social, educational, and health research contexts.
