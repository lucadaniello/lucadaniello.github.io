About Me

I am a Researcher in Social Statistics (Ricercatore a Tempo Determinato di tipo A, RTD-A) at the Department of Economics and Statistics (DiSES), University of Naples Federico II, where I hold a permanent appointment since 2025.

My scientific activity sits at the intersection of advanced statistical methods, text mining, and data science, with applications spanning the mapping of scientific knowledge, the development of decision-support tools in academic, healthcare, and economic policy contexts. I obtained my PhD in Social Sciences and Statistics (XXXVII Cycle) from the University of Naples Federico II in 2025, with a thesis titled "From Scientific Knowledge Synthesis to Cutting-Edge Discovery: The Innovative Potential of Automatic Text Summarization in Scientific Literature" (Supervisor: Prof. Rosanna Cataldo).

I am a member of the academic spin-off K-Synth and co-author of the software TALL – Text Analysis for ALL, an R package and Shiny application officially released on CRAN with over 3,000 installations. I also contribute to the development of bibliometrix, one of the leading tools for scientometric analysis globally.

With over 41 scientific publications — including papers in Fascia A international journals — and presentations at more than 20 national and international conferences, I am also active in the scientific community as Associate Editor and reviewer for leading indexed journals. I am a member of the Società Italiana di Statistica (SIS) and the Associazione Italiana di Linguistica Computazionale (AILC).
